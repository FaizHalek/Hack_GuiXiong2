1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2024, the department handled 856 cases related to remote work, of which 608 (71%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 856
- Cases resolved: 608
- Average processing time: 3.6 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 91.7%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 2,745,000
- Spent: RM 2,352,522 (86% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to equipment due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to eligibility due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for performance review by Q4 2024.
- Recruit 3 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
