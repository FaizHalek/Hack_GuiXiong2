Title: Q4 2025 Performance Report: Leave Management

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2025, the department handled 2,678 cases related to leave management, of which 1,622 (61%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,678
- Cases resolved: 1,622
- Average processing time: 5.4 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 91.7%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 18,673,000
- Spent: RM 12,690,679 (68% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to approval workflow due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to medical leave due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for emergency leave by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 6 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
