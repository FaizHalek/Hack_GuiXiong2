1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2025, the department handled 4,482 cases related to asset management, of which 3,336 (74%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,482
- Cases resolved: 3,336
- Average processing time: 5.5 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 68.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 9,044,000
- Spent: RM 5,630,392 (62% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to transfer of assets due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to write-off due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for transfer of assets by Q3 2025.
- Recruit 3 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
