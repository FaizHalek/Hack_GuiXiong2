1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2023, the department handled 1,867 cases related to travel and claims, of which 1,823 (98%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 1,867
- Cases resolved: 1,823
- Average processing time: 10.2 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 89.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 9,150,000
- Spent: RM 7,349,695 (80% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to approval levels due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to receipts due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for accommodation limits by Q4 2023.
- Recruit 4 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
