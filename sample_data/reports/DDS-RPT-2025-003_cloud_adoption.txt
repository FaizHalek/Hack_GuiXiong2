1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q2 2025, the department handled 4,812 cases related to cloud adoption, of which 3,091 (64%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,812
- Cases resolved: 3,091
- Average processing time: 14.1 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 91.4%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 2,900,000
- Spent: RM 2,385,483 (82% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to workload classification due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to cost monitoring due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for cost monitoring by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 4 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
