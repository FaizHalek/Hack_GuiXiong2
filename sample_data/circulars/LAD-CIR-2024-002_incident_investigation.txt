LAND ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 2 of 2024: Updates to Incident Investigation
Reference No.: LAD/CIR/2024/002
Date: 25 November 2024
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on incident investigation under the Workplace Safety Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2023 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 09 December 2024, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Deputy Director by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 8 of 2023.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to James Rahman at ext. 2602.

Issued by: Grace Abdullah, Head of Unit
