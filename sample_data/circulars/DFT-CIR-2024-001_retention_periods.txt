DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE AND TREASURY
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 1 of 2024: Updates to Retention Periods
Reference No.: DFT/CIR/2024/001
Date: 16 December 2024
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on retention periods under the Data Protection Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2023 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 30 December 2024, the processing period is reduced from 14 to 10 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Deputy Director by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 18 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Victor Abdullah at ext. 2730.

Issued by: Nurul Chong, Senior Officer
