MINISTRY OF EDUCATION AND CULTURE
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 4 of 2022: Updates to Receipts
Reference No.: MEC/CIR/2022/004
Date: 28 September 2022
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on receipts under the Travel and Claims Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2021 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 12 October 2022, the processing period is reduced from 30 to 14 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Head of Unit by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 16 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Kumar Chong at ext. 2194.

Issued by: Daniel Tan, Senior Officer
