POLICY
Title: Remote Work Policy
Reference No.: DHW/POL/2024/003
Date: 29 July 2024
Version: 1.6
Effective: 28 August 2024
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing remote work across the Department of Health and Wellbeing. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Performance review
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on performance review as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.2 Working hours
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on working hours as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.3 Data security at home
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on data security at home as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Aisha Osman, Deputy Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 29 July 2025.

Approved by: Nurul Tan, Deputy Director
