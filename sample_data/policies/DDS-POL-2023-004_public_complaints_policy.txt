Effective: 23 August 2023
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing public complaints across the Department of Digital Services. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Escalation
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on escalation as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.2 Satisfaction surveys
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on satisfaction surveys as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.3 Intake channels
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on intake channels as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Hafiz Osman, Deputy Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 23 July 2024.

Approved by: Grace Pillai, Deputy Director
