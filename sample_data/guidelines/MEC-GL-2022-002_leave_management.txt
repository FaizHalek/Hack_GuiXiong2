INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Leave Management Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

ANNUAL LEAVE
- Do: document every decision related to annual leave and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about annual leave.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with annual leave raised in audits relate to missing records.

APPROVAL WORKFLOW
- Do: document every decision related to approval workflow and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about approval workflow.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with approval workflow raised in audits relate to missing records.

CARRY-FORWARD
- Do: document every decision related to carry-forward and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about carry-forward.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with carry-forward raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Deputy Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 5 working days.
