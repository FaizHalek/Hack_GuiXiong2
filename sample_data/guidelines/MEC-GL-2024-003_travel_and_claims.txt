INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Travel and Claims Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

APPROVAL LEVELS
- Do: document every decision related to approval levels and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about approval levels.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with approval levels raised in audits relate to missing records.

PER DIEM RATES
- Do: document every decision related to per diem rates and keep evidence on file.

CLAIM SUBMISSION

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 9 working days.
