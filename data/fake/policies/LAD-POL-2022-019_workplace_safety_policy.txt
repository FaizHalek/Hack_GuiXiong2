1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing workplace safety across the Land Administration Department. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Incident investigation
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on incident investigation as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.2 Hazard reporting
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on hazard reporting as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.3 Fire drills
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on fire drills as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.4 First aid
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on first aid as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Daniel Lim, Senior Manager) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 09 February 2023.

Approved by: Aisha Wong, Senior Manager
