1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing procurement across the Ministry of Public Works. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Vendor registration
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on vendor registration as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Quotation thresholds
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on quotation thresholds as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.3 Payment terms
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on payment terms as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.4 Conflict of interest
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on conflict of interest as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.5 Tender evaluation
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on tender evaluation as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Victor Lee, Head of Unit) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 27 December 2023.

Approved by: Lina Osman, Senior Officer
