1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing leave management across the Department of Finance and Treasury. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Approval workflow
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on approval workflow as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.2 Annual leave
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on annual leave as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.3 Medical leave
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on medical leave as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Kumar Goh, Senior Officer) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 11 May 2025.

Approved by: Daniel Lee, Director
