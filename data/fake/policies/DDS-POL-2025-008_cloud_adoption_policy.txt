1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing cloud adoption across the Department of Digital Services. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Backup
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on backup as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Approved providers
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on approved providers as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.3 Workload classification
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on workload classification as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.4 Cost monitoring
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on cost monitoring as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 12 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Hafiz Rahman, Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 25 October 2026.

Approved by: Aisha Goh, Deputy Director
