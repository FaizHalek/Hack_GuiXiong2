POLICY
Title: Cloud Adoption Policy
Reference No.: DFT/POL/2023/004
Date: 05 December 2023
Version: 4.4
Effective: 04 January 2024
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing cloud adoption across the Department of Finance and Treasury. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Approved providers
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on approved providers as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Exit planning
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on exit planning as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.3 Cost monitoring
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on cost monitoring as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.4 Workload classification
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on workload classification as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.5 Backup
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on backup as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (James Pillai, Senior Manager) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 04 December 2024.

Approved by: Hafiz Ibrahim, Head of Unit
