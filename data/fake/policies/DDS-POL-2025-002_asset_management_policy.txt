1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing asset management across the Department of Digital Services. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Asset tagging
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on asset tagging as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Annual stock take
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on annual stock take as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.3 Write-off
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on write-off as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Grace Osman, Deputy Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 14 April 2026.

Approved by: Daniel Goh, Head of Unit
