1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing public complaints across the Ministry of Public Works. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Case closure
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on case closure as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Satisfaction surveys
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on satisfaction surveys as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.3 Response times
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on response times as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.4 Intake channels
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on intake channels as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.5 Escalation
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on escalation as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 24 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Kumar Osman, Senior Manager) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 26 July 2026.

Approved by: Mei Ling Lee, Head of Unit
