POLICY
Title: Travel and Claims Policy

Date: 02 February 2024

Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing travel and claims across the Land Administration Department. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Approval levels
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on approval levels as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.2 Receipts
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on receipts as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.3 Accommodation limits
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on accommodation limits as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.4 Claim submission
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on claim submission as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.5 Per diem rates
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on per diem rates as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Mei Ling Pillai, Deputy Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 01 February 2025.

Approved by: Daniel Goh, Senior Manager
