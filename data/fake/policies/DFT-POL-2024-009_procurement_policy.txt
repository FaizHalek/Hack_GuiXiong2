POLICY
Title: Procurement Policy

Date: 15 January 2024
Version: 1.1
Effective: 14 February 2024
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing procurement across the Department of Finance and Treasury. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Payment terms
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on payment terms as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Officer and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.2 Conflict of interest
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on conflict of interest as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.3 Quotation thresholds
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on quotation thresholds as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 12 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Daniel Tan, Principal Assistant Secretary) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 14 January 2025.

Approved by: Kumar Ibrahim, Principal Assistant Secretary
