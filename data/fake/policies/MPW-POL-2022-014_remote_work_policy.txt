POLICY
Title: Remote Work Policy

Date: 30 January 2022
Version: 1.8

Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing remote work across the Ministry of Public Works. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Equipment
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on equipment as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Data security at home
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on data security at home as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.3 Eligibility
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on eligibility as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Deputy Director and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.4 Working hours
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on working hours as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Senior Manager and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.5 Performance review
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on performance review as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of Unit and must be reviewed every 12 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Mei Ling Rahman, Senior Officer) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 30 January 2023.

Approved by: James Ibrahim, Senior Officer
