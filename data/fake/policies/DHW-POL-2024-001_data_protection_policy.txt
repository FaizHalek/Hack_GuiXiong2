POLICY
Title: Data Protection Policy
Reference No.: DHW/POL/2024/001
Date: 19 March 2024
Version: 1.1
Effective: 18 April 2024
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out the principles and requirements governing data protection across the Department of Health and Wellbeing. It aims to ensure consistency, accountability and compliance with applicable regulations.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent, contract and temporary staff, as well as consultants engaged by the department.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 Data classification
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on data classification as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.2 Retention periods
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on retention periods as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 12 months.
3.3 Breach reporting
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on breach reporting as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 6 months.
3.4 Access control
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on access control as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Director and must be reviewed every 24 months.
3.5 Personal data handling
    All staff shall comply with the requirements on personal data handling as described in this section. Exceptions require written approval from the Principal Assistant Secretary and must be reviewed every 6 months.

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- Heads of Unit are responsible for enforcing this policy within their units.
- The Policy Owner (Hafiz Tan, Deputy Director) is responsible for reviewing this policy annually.
- All staff are responsible for reading and complying with this policy.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Public Service Conduct Regulations.

6. REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed no later than 19 March 2025.

Approved by: Lina Osman, Head of Unit
