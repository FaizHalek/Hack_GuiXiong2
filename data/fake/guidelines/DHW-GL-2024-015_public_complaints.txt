INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Public Complaints Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

INTAKE CHANNELS
- Do: document every decision related to intake channels and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about intake channels.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with intake channels raised in audits relate to missing records.

ESCALATION
- Do: document every decision related to escalation and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about escalation.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with escalation raised in audits relate to missing records.

CASE CLOSURE
- Do: document every decision related to case closure and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about case closure.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with case closure raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Deputy Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 9 working days.
