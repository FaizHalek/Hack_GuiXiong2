INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Procurement Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

QUOTATION THRESHOLDS
- Do: document every decision related to quotation thresholds and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about quotation thresholds.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with quotation thresholds raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Senior Officer of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 10 working days.
