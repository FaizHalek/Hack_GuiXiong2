INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Asset Management Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

ASSET TAGGING
- Do: document every decision related to asset tagging and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about asset tagging.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with asset tagging raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Principal Assistant Secretary of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 10 working days.
