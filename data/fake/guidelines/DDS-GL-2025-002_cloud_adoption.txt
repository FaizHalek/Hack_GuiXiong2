INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Cloud Adoption Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

EXIT PLANNING
- Do: document every decision related to exit planning and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about exit planning.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with exit planning raised in audits relate to missing records.
