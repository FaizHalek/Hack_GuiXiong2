INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Asset Management Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

WRITE-OFF
- Do: document every decision related to write-off and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about write-off.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with write-off raised in audits relate to missing records.

ASSET TAGGING
- Do: document every decision related to asset tagging and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about asset tagging.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with asset tagging raised in audits relate to missing records.

TRANSFER OF ASSETS
- Do: document every decision related to transfer of assets and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about transfer of assets.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with transfer of assets raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Head of Unit of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 5 working days.
