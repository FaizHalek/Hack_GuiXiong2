INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Remote Work Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

PERFORMANCE REVIEW
- Do: document every decision related to performance review and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about performance review.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with performance review raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 8 working days.
