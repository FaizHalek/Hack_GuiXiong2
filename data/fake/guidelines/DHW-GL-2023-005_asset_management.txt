INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Asset Management Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

TRANSFER OF ASSETS
- Do: document every decision related to transfer of assets and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about transfer of assets.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with transfer of assets raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Senior Manager of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 6 working days.
