INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Travel and Claims Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

PER DIEM RATES
- Do: document every decision related to per diem rates and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about per diem rates.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with per diem rates raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Deputy Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 8 working days.
