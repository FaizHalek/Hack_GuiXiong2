INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Remote Work Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

EQUIPMENT
- Do: document every decision related to equipment and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about equipment.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with equipment raised in audits relate to missing records.

DATA SECURITY AT HOME
- Do: document every decision related to data security at home and keep evidence on file.

- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with data security at home raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 9 working days.
