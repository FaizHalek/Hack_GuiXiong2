INTRODUCTION
These guidelines provide practical advice to help staff apply the Procurement Policy. They are advisory and should be read together with the relevant policy and SOPs.

CONFLICT OF INTEREST
- Do: document every decision related to conflict of interest and keep evidence on file.
- Do: consult your Head of Unit when in doubt about conflict of interest.
- Don't: bypass the approval workflow, even for urgent cases.
- Tip: most issues with conflict of interest raised in audits relate to missing records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Who approves exceptions?
A: The Deputy Director of the relevant division.
Q: How long does approval take?
A: Typically 3 working days.
