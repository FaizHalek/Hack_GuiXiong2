REPORT
Title: Q1 2022 Performance Report: Data Protection
Reference No.: MPW/RPT/2022/012

Prepared by: Daniel Osman, Deputy Director
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2022, the department handled 2,195 cases related to data protection, of which 2,041 (93%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,195
- Cases resolved: 2,041
- Average processing time: 13.3 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 88.5%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 5,392,000
- Spent: RM 4,154,599 (77% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to breach reporting due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to retention periods due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for data classification by Q3 2022.
- Recruit 2 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
