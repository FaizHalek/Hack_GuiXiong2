1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q3 2022, the department handled 3,137 cases related to procurement, of which 2,763 (88%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 3,137
- Cases resolved: 2,763
- Average processing time: 10.5 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 66.2%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 12,471,000
- Spent: RM 6,921,752 (56% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to vendor registration due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to quotation thresholds due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for tender evaluation by Q4 2022.
- Recruit 10 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
