1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q3 2025, the department handled 3,147 cases related to remote work, of which 2,815 (89%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 3,147
- Cases resolved: 2,815
- Average processing time: 14.9 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 83.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 18,692,000
- Spent: RM 15,194,194 (81% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to data security at home due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to working hours due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for working hours by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 8 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
