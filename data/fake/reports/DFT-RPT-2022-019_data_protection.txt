1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2022, the department handled 469 cases related to data protection, of which 287 (61%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 469
- Cases resolved: 287
- Average processing time: 11.1 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 65.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 4,166,000
- Spent: RM 2,349,653 (56% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to personal data handling due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to data classification due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for data classification by Q4 2022.
- Recruit 2 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
