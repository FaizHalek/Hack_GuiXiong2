LAND ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT
============================================================
REPORT
Title: Q4 2023 Performance Report: Leave Management
Reference No.: LAD/RPT/2023/013
Date: 19 December 2023
Prepared by: Rajesh Wong, Director
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2023, the department handled 4,938 cases related to leave management, of which 3,870 (78%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,938
- Cases resolved: 3,870
- Average processing time: 2.6 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 80.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 17,460,000
- Spent: RM 16,414,380 (94% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to carry-forward due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to medical leave due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for medical leave by Q4 2023.
- Recruit 4 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
