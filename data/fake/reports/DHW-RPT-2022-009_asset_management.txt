1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2022, the department handled 3,151 cases related to asset management, of which 2,567 (81%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 3,151
- Cases resolved: 2,567
- Average processing time: 14.5 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 73.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 3,166,000
- Spent: RM 2,423,158 (77% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to loss reporting due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to transfer of assets due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for transfer of assets by Q4 2022.
- Recruit 7 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
