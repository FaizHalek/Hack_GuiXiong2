DEPARTMENT OF DIGITAL SERVICES

REPORT
Title: Q1 2022 Performance Report: Remote Work
Reference No.: DDS/RPT/2022/011
Date: 06 January 2022
Prepared by: James Lee, Deputy Director
Classification: Internal

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2022, the department handled 4,845 cases related to remote work, of which 3,633 (75%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,845
- Cases resolved: 3,633
- Average processing time: 7.4 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 74.0%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 10,452,000
- Spent: RM 5,893,021 (56% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to equipment due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to performance review due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for working hours by Q3 2022.
- Recruit 9 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
