1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q2 2023, the department handled 2,813 cases related to leave management, of which 2,449 (87%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,813
- Cases resolved: 2,449
- Average processing time: 14.2 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 85.4%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 1,706,000
- Spent: RM 1,056,637 (62% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to carry-forward due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to medical leave due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for emergency leave by Q4 2023.
- Recruit 3 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
