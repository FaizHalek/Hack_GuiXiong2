REPORT
Title: Q2 2024 Performance Report: Leave Management
Reference No.: MEC/RPT/2024/003

Prepared by: Kumar Ibrahim, Deputy Director
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q2 2024, the department handled 1,664 cases related to leave management, of which 1,021 (61%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 1,664
- Cases resolved: 1,021
- Average processing time: 7.8 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 83.6%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 614,000
- Spent: RM 534,999 (87% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to carry-forward due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to medical leave due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for carry-forward by Q4 2024.
- Recruit 8 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
