Title: Q3 2025 Performance Report: Remote Work

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q3 2025, the department handled 2,855 cases related to remote work, of which 1,954 (68%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,855
- Cases resolved: 1,954
- Average processing time: 10.4 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 74.2%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 13,274,000
- Spent: RM 11,411,815 (86% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to equipment due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to data security at home due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for data security at home by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 7 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
