1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2024, the department handled 1,832 cases related to asset management, of which 1,645 (90%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 1,832
- Cases resolved: 1,645
- Average processing time: 5.2 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 71.1%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 2,660,000
- Spent: RM 2,734,024 (103% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to asset tagging due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to loss reporting due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for asset tagging by Q3 2024.
- Recruit 6 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
