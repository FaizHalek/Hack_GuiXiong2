1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2022, the department handled 4,713 cases related to procurement, of which 4,172 (89%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,713
- Cases resolved: 4,172
- Average processing time: 10.4 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 84.4%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 6,963,000
- Spent: RM 4,075,861 (59% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to conflict of interest due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to vendor registration due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for payment terms by Q3 2022.
- Recruit 6 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
