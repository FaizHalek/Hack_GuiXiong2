1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2022, the department handled 1,590 cases related to leave management, of which 1,220 (77%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 1,590
- Cases resolved: 1,220
- Average processing time: 13.9 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 70.8%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 8,236,000
- Spent: RM 8,277,457 (101% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to carry-forward due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to emergency leave due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for approval workflow by Q3 2022.
- Recruit 5 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
