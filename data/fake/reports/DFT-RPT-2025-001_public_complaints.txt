Title: Q2 2025 Performance Report: Public Complaints

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q2 2025, the department handled 1,806 cases related to public complaints, of which 1,567 (87%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 1,806
- Cases resolved: 1,567
- Average processing time: 4.0 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 75.8%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 6,495,000
- Spent: RM 5,450,970 (84% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to response times due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to satisfaction surveys due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for response times by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 2 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
