1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q3 2025, the department handled 517 cases related to travel and claims, of which 341 (66%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 517
- Cases resolved: 341
- Average processing time: 2.7 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 84.1%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 11,494,000
- Spent: RM 11,893,581 (103% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to claim submission due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to accommodation limits due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for accommodation limits by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 5 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
