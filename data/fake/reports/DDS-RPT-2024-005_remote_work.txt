1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q4 2024, the department handled 3,295 cases related to remote work, of which 2,255 (68%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 3,295
- Cases resolved: 2,255
- Average processing time: 12.6 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 70.8%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 1,347,000
- Spent: RM 1,366,184 (101% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to data security at home due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to working hours due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for equipment by Q4 2024.
- Recruit 5 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
