Title: Q2 2025 Performance Report: Workplace Safety

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q2 2025, the department handled 2,266 cases related to workplace safety, of which 1,521 (67%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,266
- Cases resolved: 1,521
- Average processing time: 5.3 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 93.9%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 11,111,000
- Spent: RM 11,532,355 (104% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to incident investigation due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to fire drills due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for protective equipment by Q4 2025.
- Recruit 6 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
