1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q1 2022, the department handled 2,015 cases related to remote work, of which 1,361 (68%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 2,015
- Cases resolved: 1,361
- Average processing time: 6.7 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 66.4%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 10,034,000
- Spent: RM 8,725,879 (87% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to data security at home due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to equipment due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for performance review by Q3 2022.
- Recruit 2 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
