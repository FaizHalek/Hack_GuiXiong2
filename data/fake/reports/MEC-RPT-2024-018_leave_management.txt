Title: Q3 2024 Performance Report: Leave Management

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Q3 2024, the department handled 4,067 cases related to leave management, of which 3,526 (87%) were resolved within the service standard.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
- Cases received: 4,067
- Cases resolved: 3,526
- Average processing time: 9.4 working days
- Customer satisfaction: 74.2%

3. BUDGET
- Allocated: RM 709,000
- Spent: RM 719,290 (101% utilisation)

4. ISSUES AND RISKS
- Delays related to medical leave due to staffing constraints.
- Delays related to carry-forward due to staffing constraints.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Digitise remaining manual processes for approval workflow by Q4 2024.
- Recruit 3 additional officers to reduce backlog.
- Conduct refresher training for all units.
