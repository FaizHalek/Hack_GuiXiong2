DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND WELLBEING
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 19 of 2025: Updates to Performance Review
Reference No.: DHW/CIR/2025/019
Date: 22 July 2025
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on performance review under the Remote Work Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2024 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 05 August 2025, the processing period is reduced from 30 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Deputy Director by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 4 of 2023.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Victor Ibrahim at ext. 2976.

Issued by: Siti Lim, Principal Assistant Secretary
