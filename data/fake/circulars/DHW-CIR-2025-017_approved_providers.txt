DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND WELLBEING
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 17 of 2025: Updates to Approved Providers
Reference No.: DHW/CIR/2025/017
Date: 17 July 2025
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on approved providers under the Cloud Adoption Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2024 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 31 July 2025, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Officer by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 13 of 2024.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Siti Rahman at ext. 2475.

Issued by: Daniel Rahman, Senior Manager
