LAND ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 8 of 2025: Updates to Access Logs
Reference No.: LAD/CIR/2025/008
Date: 27 July 2025
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on access logs under the Records Management Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2024 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 10 August 2025, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Director by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 3 of 2023.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Grace Goh at ext. 2196.

Issued by: Siti Lim, Deputy Director
