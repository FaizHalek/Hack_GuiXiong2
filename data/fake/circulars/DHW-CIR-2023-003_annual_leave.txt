DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND WELLBEING
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 3 of 2023: Updates to Annual Leave
Reference No.: DHW/CIR/2023/003
Date: 16 October 2023
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on annual leave under the Leave Management Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2022 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 30 October 2023, the processing period is reduced from 7 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Manager by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 19 of 2022.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Aisha Pillai at ext. 2891.

Issued by: Siti Chong, Principal Assistant Secretary
