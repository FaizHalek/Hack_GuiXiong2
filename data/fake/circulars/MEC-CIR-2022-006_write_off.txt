MINISTRY OF EDUCATION AND CULTURE
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 6 of 2022: Updates to Write-Off
Reference No.: MEC/CIR/2022/006
Date: 01 January 2022
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on write-off under the Asset Management Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2021 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 15 January 2022, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Head of Unit by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 15 of 2020.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Rajesh Tan at ext. 2229.

Issued by: Mei Ling Wong, Head of Unit
