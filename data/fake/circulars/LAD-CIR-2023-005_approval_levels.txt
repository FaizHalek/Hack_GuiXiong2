LAND ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 5 of 2023: Updates to Approval Levels
Reference No.: LAD/CIR/2023/005
Date: 10 July 2023
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on approval levels under the Travel and Claims Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2022 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 24 July 2023, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Principal Assistant Secretary by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 15 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Nurul Rahman at ext. 2130.

Issued by: Aisha Chong, Senior Officer
