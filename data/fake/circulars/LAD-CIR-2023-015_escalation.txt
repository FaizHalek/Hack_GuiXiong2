LAND ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 15 of 2023: Updates to Escalation
Reference No.: LAD/CIR/2023/015
Date: 11 November 2023
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on escalation under the Public Complaints Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2022 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 25 November 2023, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Director by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 1 of 2022.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Nurul Tan at ext. 2359.

Issued by: Siti Tan, Head of Unit
