MINISTRY OF EDUCATION AND CULTURE
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 20 of 2023: Updates to Breach Reporting
Reference No.: MEC/CIR/2023/020
Date: 10 January 2023
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on breach reporting under the Data Protection Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2022 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 24 January 2023, the processing period is reduced from 7 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Officer by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 5 of 2020.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Rajesh Nair at ext. 2881.

Issued by: James Ibrahim, Senior Manager
