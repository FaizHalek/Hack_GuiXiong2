CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 14 of 2022: Updates to Cost Monitoring
Reference No.: MEC/CIR/2022/014
Date: 20 August 2022
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on cost monitoring under the Cloud Adoption Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2021 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 03 September 2022, the processing period is reduced from 5 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Manager by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 7 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Lina Lee at ext. 2764.

Issued by: Kumar Rahman, Senior Manager
