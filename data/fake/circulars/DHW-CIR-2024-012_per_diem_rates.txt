DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND WELLBEING
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 12 of 2024: Updates to Per Diem Rates
Reference No.: DHW/CIR/2024/012
Date: 10 August 2024
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on per diem rates under the Travel and Claims Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2023 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 24 August 2024, the processing period is reduced from 7 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Manager by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 4 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Kumar Goh at ext. 2191.

Issued by: Rajesh Ibrahim, Principal Assistant Secretary
