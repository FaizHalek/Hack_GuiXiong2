DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE AND TREASURY
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 9 of 2025: Updates to Eligibility
Reference No.: DFT/CIR/2025/009
Date: 05 January 2025
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on eligibility under the Remote Work Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2024 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 19 January 2025, the processing period is reduced from 10 to 7 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Senior Manager by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 11 of 2022.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Rajesh Ibrahim at ext. 2538.

Issued by: Rajesh Lee, Deputy Director
