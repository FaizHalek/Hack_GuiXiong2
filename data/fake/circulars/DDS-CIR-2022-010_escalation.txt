DEPARTMENT OF DIGITAL SERVICES
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 10 of 2022: Updates to Escalation
Reference No.: DDS/CIR/2022/010
Date: 24 April 2022
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on escalation under the Public Complaints Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2021 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 08 May 2022, the processing period is reduced from 5 to 3 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Principal Assistant Secretary by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 14 of 2021.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Nurul Abdullah at ext. 2683.

Issued by: Siti Nair, Deputy Director
