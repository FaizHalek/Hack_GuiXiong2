DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND WELLBEING
============================================================
CIRCULAR
Title: Circular No. 1 of 2025: Updates to Digitisation
Reference No.: DHW/CIR/2025/001
Date: 05 September 2025
To: All Heads of Department and Staff
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. PURPOSE
This circular informs all staff of changes to requirements on digitisation under the Records Management Policy.

2. BACKGROUND
A review conducted in 2024 found inconsistencies in how units apply the current requirements, resulting in delays and audit findings.

3. CHANGES
3.1 Effective 19 September 2025, the processing period is reduced from 7 to 5 working days.
3.2 All submissions must be made through the internal portal. Paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
3.3 Units must report monthly compliance figures to the Principal Assistant Secretary by the 5th of each month.

4. SUPERSESSION
This circular supersedes Circular No. 18 of 2022.

5. ENQUIRIES
Enquiries may be directed to Siti Tan at ext. 2171.

Issued by: Lina Wong, Director
