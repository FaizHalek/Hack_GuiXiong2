STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Standard Operating Procedure for Data Classification (Data Protection)
Reference No.: DDS/SOP/2022/005
Date: 25 May 2022
Related Policy: DDS/POL/2020/009
Classification: Internal
============================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
To describe the step-by-step procedure for data classification under the Data Protection framework.

2. DEFINITIONS
- Requester: the officer initiating the process.
- Approver: the officer with delegated authority to approve the request.
- Unit Coordinator: the officer who maintains records for the unit.

3. PROCEDURE
Step 1: The Requester completes the relevant form in the internal portal.
Step 2: The Requester attaches supporting documents and submits the form.
Step 3: The Unit Coordinator verifies completeness within 2 working days.
Step 4: The Approver reviews and approves or rejects the request within 3 working days.
Step 5: If rejected, the Approver records the reason and returns the request to the Requester.
Step 6: Upon approval, the Unit Coordinator updates the register and notifies the Requester by email.
Step 7: Records are retained for 3 years in accordance with the Records Management Policy.

4. TURNAROUND TIME
The end-to-end process shall not exceed 6 working days.

5. FORMS AND TEMPLATES
- Form DDS-781: Request Form
- Form DDS-961: Approval Checklist
